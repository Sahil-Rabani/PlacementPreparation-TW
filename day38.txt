## 👨‍🏫 Coach's Note

This week is laying the foundation for **Week 5: Pointers**, which is one of the most important topics in C.

As you solve today's problems, don't just aim for correct output. For every solution, also think about:

* What is the **time complexity**?
* What is the **space complexity**?
* What **edge cases** could break the solution?
* Can the algorithm be made simpler or more efficient?
