# Week 3 – Day 7

## Revision + Weekly Test Preparation

No major new C topic today.

**Total: 2 hours 45 minutes**

### C Revision — 90 minutes

Without looking at previous solutions, write these:

1. Recursive GCD using Euclid's algorithm.
2. Recursive decimal → binary.
3. Reverse an array **in-place**.
4. Find second-largest **distinct** element. Test:

   * `[5,8,3,8,7] → 7`
   * `[-5,-10,-2,-3] → -3`
   * `[4,4,4] → No second largest`
5. Left-rotate an array by `k`.
6. Find maximum recursively.

Then answer these conceptual questions:

* Why is `arr[n]` invalid when an array contains `n` elements?
* Why is array access normally O(1)?
* Difference between `O(n)`, `O(log n)` and `O(n²)`.
* Why does `sizeof(arr)/sizeof(arr[0])` work in `main()` but may fail after passing `arr` to a function?
* Difference between stack memory and the call stack used during recursion.

### Aptitude — 30 minutes

1. A father is four times his son's age. Eight years later, he will be 2.5 times the son's age. Find present ages.
2. A can complete work in 15 days and B in 10 days. They work together for 4 days. What fraction of the work remains?
3. A 200 m train crosses a 300 m platform in 20 seconds. Find its speed in km/h.
4. Two trains 180 m and 270 m long move in the same direction at 72 km/h and 54 km/h. Find the time for the faster train to completely overtake the slower one.
5. A person travels equal distances at 30, 40 and 60 km/h. Find the average speed for the complete journey.

### Reasoning — 20 minutes

1. `2, 5, 12, 27, 58, ?`
2. `3, 10, 29, 66, 127, ?`
3. If `ARRAY → BSSBZ`, encode `STACK`.
4. A woman says, **"The man's father's only daughter is my mother."** How is the man related to the woman?
5. Six people A–F sit in a row. B is immediately right of A. D is immediately left of E. C sits somewhere between B and D. F is right of E. Give one valid arrangement.

### Debugging Challenge

Explain everything wrong with:

```c
int findMax(int arr[], int n)
{
    int max = arr[0];

    for(int i = 0; i <= n; i++)
    {
        if(arr[i] > max)
            max = arr[i];
    }

    return max;
}
```

Then give:

* corrected code,
* time complexity,
* space complexity,
* what happens when `n == 0`.
