# Debugging Challenge

Predict what is wrong:

```c
int arr[5] = {10,20,30,40,50};

for(int i = 0; i < 5; i++)
{
    printf("%d ", arr[i+1]);
}
```

Answer:

1. Which iterations are valid?
2. When does out-of-bounds access happen?
3. Why might the program still appear to work?
4. Correct it.

# Aptitude — 30 Minutes

Show complete working.

**Q1 — Ages:** A father is three times his son's age. Ten years ago, he was five times his son's age. Find their present ages.

**Q2 — Time & Work:** A can complete work in 20 days and B in 30 days. They work together for 6 days. Then A leaves. How many more days does B require?

**Q3 — Trains:** Two trains, 180 m and 220 m long, travel in opposite directions at 54 km/h and 90 km/h. Find their crossing time.

**Q4 — Average Speed:** A car travels 120 km at 40 km/h, 180 km at 60 km/h, and 100 km at 50 km/h. Find its average speed for the complete journey.

**Q5 — Catch-up:** A cyclist leaves at 7:00 AM at 15 km/h. Another cyclist starts from the same point at 8:30 AM at 24 km/h. At what time does the second cyclist catch the first?
