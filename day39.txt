## Coding

### Q1

Input and print a `3 × 3` matrix.

### Q2

Find the sum of all matrix elements.

### Q3

Find the largest element in a matrix.

### Q4

Find the sum of each row.

Example:

```text
1 2 3
4 5 6
7 8 9

Row 1 = 6
Row 2 = 15
Row 3 = 24
```

### Q5

Find the **main diagonal sum**.

For:

```text
1 2 3
4 5 6
7 8 9
```

answer:

```text
1 + 5 + 9 = 15
```

### ⭐ Q6

Find the **transpose** of a matrix.

```text
1 2 3
4 5 6
```

↓

```text
1 4
2 5
3 6
```

### One important correction from today's work

You're getting very good at writing algorithms, but I want you to stop saying **"compiler error"** whenever something is wrong.

From now on, classify bugs as:

* **Syntax/compile-time error**
* **Runtime error**
* **Undefined behavior**
* **Logical error**

That distinction is especially important for **C interviews**.
